# Security Policy

Globalized builds financial infrastructure on regulated rails, and we take the security of our systems and our customers' assets seriously. We appreciate the work of security researchers and will engage with you in good faith.

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Please do not report security vulnerabilities through public GitHub issues, discussions or pull requests.**

Email **contact@globalized.io** with the subject line `Security report`. Include:

- A description of the issue and its potential impact
- Steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept
- The affected domain, endpoint or component
- Your contact details, if you would like credit or follow-up

We will acknowledge your report within 3 business days and keep you informed as we investigate and remediate.

## Scope

- `*.globalized.io`
- Public repositories under the `globalized-io` GitHub organization

Third-party providers integrated into the Globalized platform are out of scope. Please report issues in their systems to them directly.

## Guidelines

To protect our customers and keep your research lawful, please:

- Only test against accounts you own or have explicit permission to use
- Do not access, modify or exfiltrate data that is not yours
- Do not perform denial-of-service, spam or social-engineering attacks
- Stop testing and report immediately if you encounter sensitive data
- Give us reasonable time to remediate before any public disclosure

Research conducted in line with this policy is authorized. We will not pursue legal action against researchers who act in good faith.
